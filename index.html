Key dates

  1. 0120 and 22 October 2026

    Strategic asset allocation

    We allocate the capital across broad asset classes with long-only ETFs and present the allocation, its risk budget and its backtest.

  2. 0210 and 12 November 2026

    Discretionary equity

    We deviate from the allocation with individual stocks, chosen on valuation and on catalysts expected before the end of the year.

  3. 031 and 3 December 2026

    Quantitative and macro

    We add a factor-based strategy and a macro strategy, and we decide which of our stocks to keep.

  4. 0427 December 2026

    End of the challenge

    The portfolio is held unchanged to the end. The ranking is computed on the Sharpe Ratio realised over the whole period.

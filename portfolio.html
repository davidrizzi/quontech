Portfolio Approach

We manage USD 100 million, long-only, in three rounds. The first round sets a strategic asset allocation and fixes it for the rest of the challenge; the second and third deviate from it tactically, each within a stated budget of active risk. Performance is measured by the Sharpe Ratio of the returns realised from the start of the first round to 27 December 2026.

01

First round

Strategic asset allocation

We allocate the full USD 100 million across broad asset classes with long-only ETFs: the long-run allocation we consider most appropriate, built to an annual volatility target of 8%. The weights come from an explicit method, and the allocation is backtested with monthly rebalancing. This allocation does not change again: it is the reference against which everything we do later is measured.

Presented 20 and 22 October 2026

02

Second round

Discretionary equity investing

We sell part of the allocation and invest the proceeds in individual stocks, chosen on an explicit valuation and on catalysts we expect before the end of the year. The deviation is sized by a tracking-error budget against our own allocation, between 2% and 5% per year, so that the active risk stays deliberate rather than accidental.

Presented 10 and 12 November 2026

03

Third round

Quantitative and macro strategies

We add two tactical strategies: one quantitative, built on factors such as value, momentum, quality or low beta, and one macro, expressed through the yield curve, currencies, commodities or a global theme. Total ex ante tracking error against our allocation stays at or below 7% per year, and every stock from the second round is kept or closed on the evidence.

Presented 1 and 3 December 2026

Risk framework

Volatility target

The strategic allocation targets 8% annual volatility, with ex ante volatility between 7% and 9%, computed from the covariance matrix of monthly USD returns over the last sixty months and annualised.

Value at Risk

We translate that risk into a one-month Value at Risk at the 5% level, reported both as a percentage of the portfolio and in dollars, and we compare realised volatility with the target at every round.

Active risk

Tactical deviations are measured as tracking error against our own allocation: 2% to 5% per year in the second round, at most 7% in the third, including the positions we carry over.

Performance

Updated after each round, on the returns realised since the start of the challenge.

Company profile

QuonTech Asset Management is the investment team of Group 8 in the USI Lugano Asset Management Challenge 2026. We manage a USD 100 million long-only portfolio, measured against the Bloomberg Global EQ:FI 60:40 Index and ranked on its information ratio. The challenge has three rounds: a strategic allocation with broad-asset-class ETFs, a discretionary equity round in which we select individual stocks, and a final round built on quantitative and macro strategies. Our goal is to add value over the benchmark with controlled active risk and to explain every decision we take.

Our approach

Quantitative Precision

We use optimization, risk budgeting, factor signals and backtesting to set weights and to understand where our risk comes from.

Fundamental and Macro Insight

We value companies with the methods seen in class, look for catalysts that can re-rate them before year-end, and link every position to our view on growth, inflation and central banks.

Disciplined Risk Control

We manage tracking error against the benchmark, set position limits, watch drawdowns and follow the rules we set.

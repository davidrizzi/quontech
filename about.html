Company profile

QuonTech Asset Management is the investment team of Group 8 in the Asset Management Challenge 2026, part of the Financial Modeling course at USI Lugano. We manage a long-only portfolio of USD 100 million from October to the end of December 2026. The portfolio is built in three rounds: a strategic asset allocation that stays fixed until the end of the challenge, and two tactical deviations from it. Performance is measured by the Sharpe Ratio of the realised returns, with the one-month US Treasury bill as the risk-free rate. Our goal is to earn that return inside a risk budget we state in advance, and to be able to explain every position we hold.

Our approach

Quantitative Precision

We set the weights with optimisation and risk budgeting, we measure the risk contribution of every position, and we backtest the allocation with monthly rebalancing before we commit capital to it.

Fundamental and Macro Insight

We value companies with the methods of the course, we look for catalysts that can re-rate them within our horizon, and we link every tactical position to a view on growth, inflation and central banks.

Disciplined Risk Control

The allocation is built to a volatility target, the deviations are limited by a tracking-error budget against it, and both are reported ex ante, not justified after the fact.

Investment policy at a glance

CapitalUSD 100 million, allocated at inception. Cash earns no interest.
UniverseGlobal equities and exchange traded products. The first round uses only ETFs on broad asset classes; individual stocks enter from the second round.
PositionsLong-only in every round. No short selling and no borrowing. The number of positions is kept deliberately low.
ObjectiveThe highest Sharpe Ratio we can earn inside our risk budget, with the one-month US Treasury bill as the risk-free rate.
Risk budget8% annual volatility target on the strategic allocation, with ex ante volatility between 7% and 9%, and a one-month 5% Value at Risk reported in percent and in dollars.
Active riskEx ante tracking error against our own allocation: between 2% and 5% in the second round, at most 7% in the third.
ReferenceOur strategic asset allocation, fixed in the first round. The challenge sets no common benchmark.
HorizonFrom the first round to 27 December 2026. The portfolio is rebalanced once per round and held unchanged in between.
ReportingA technical report, a full portfolio description and a public presentation at every round, published on this website.
